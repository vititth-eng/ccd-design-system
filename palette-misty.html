Misty palette · mostly-from-Figma

All surface, brand, and tint colors come from figma.com/colors/misty-blue/. The only exceptions are three saturated status dots (olive, amber, rust) used only inside status pills — the misty page has no saturated red/green/yellow, so status pills get a documented escape hatch. Meaning carried by background tint hue, text is always ink.

3 · Meaning carried by tint hue (no foreground colors)

Default = white card + ink. To mark meaning, change only the background tint. Text stays ink. The hue of the bg signals the state.

TintHueUse for
tint-positivepale green-yellowlive, success, done, completed
tint-cautioncream (warm yellow)pending, scheduled, awaiting · soft heads-up
tint-alertpeach (warm orange-red)expired, at-risk, failed · hard warning, needs action

Status pills · four semantic groups

Pastel bg carries the calm; a small saturated dot carries the meaning. Positive (live, done), neutral (beta, draft, archived), caution (pending, scheduled — soft heads-up), negative (expired, at-risk, failed — needs action).
