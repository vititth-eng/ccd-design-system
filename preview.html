Brand

Three blues from the misty-blue scale. Deep is identity (Singha lockup, primary text). Mid is action (links, focus, brand CTA). Soft is anchor (selected state, calm surface). One family, three jobs.

Logo lockup · navy wordmark on paper is canonical

Deep panel demo — verifies wordmark legibility on dark surface (footer, splash).

Role split — what each blue does
Deep #142549identity, primary text, footer, institutional headers
Mid #4874CDprimary action, links, focus, brand CTA
Soft #B5C7EBanchor misty · selected state, current chip, calm bg
Paper-tint #F6F9FDsoftest wash · outer canvas, card icon bg, neutral pill

One family, no warm accent. The misty system carries no amber, no green, no red. Meaning lives in pastel tints (positive / caution / alert) — see the Color section.
