Higher contrast and completely neutral. The blue button becomes the only colour on the screen, which makes it louder. Boonrawd navy survives only as the brand mark, not as the reading colour.

Worth knowing: #142549 is named in colors.css as “the fixed brand anchor”, and it is the one value in the palette that is Boonrawd's rather than a design choice. Option B does not delete it — it demotes it from the colour text is set in to a colour used for the brand. That is a real change in what the apps look like, and it is not reversible cheaply once screens are built on it.

The cost, and it is a real one

CCD's whole surface strategy is flat white separated by line — cards, popovers and the page are all #FFFFFF, so the border is the only thing making a card a card. shadcn's border is lighter than CCD's: 1.26:1 against 1.53:1.

shadcn's dark page is #0A0A0A — near-black, against CCD's #191A1D. More noticeably, its border is oklch(1 0 0 / 10%): white at 10% opacity, not a solid grey. That is the same alpha architecture found in its components, and it is genuinely better here — one border value lands correctly on the page, on a card, and inside a dialog, where a solid grey is right at exactly one depth.

5 · Recommendation

Take the swap. Keep navy. Hold the border.

Take shadcn's neutral ramp for muted, muted-foreground, secondary, accent and the dark surfaces, and adopt its alpha borders in dark mode — that last one fixes a problem CCD has today, where one solid grey has to serve a page, a card and a dialog.

Keep #142549 as --foreground. It is the only Boonrawd-owned value in the palette, it is what makes CCD screens not look like every other shadcn app, and the thing gained by dropping it — 19.80:1 instead of 15.10:1 — is contrast nobody was short of.

Keep the border at CCD's weight, #CBD1DF rather than #E5E5E5. Flat-white-separated-by-line only works if the line is visible, and shadcn's is a third weaker because its cards are allowed a background CCD's are not.

Keep --destructive at #C4372C rather than shadcn's #E7000B, and keep the status trio — shadcn has no slot for two of the three, and CCD's red was measured for text and fills, which shadcn's was not.

Net: the neutral family goes from blue-grey to true grey, the navy and the brand blue stay, the border holds, and dark mode gains alpha borders. That answers the open chroma-inconsistency question by picking flat — both modes now agree.
