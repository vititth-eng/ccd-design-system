v3 — the base palette, measured

Not the chart tier. The tokens every screen touches: the neutrals, the navy, the action blue, the lines, the status trio. All of it was carried over from v2 without being re-judged, and this is the first time it has been measured as a system rather than one colour at a time.

1 · Too much, or too small?

Neither. It is the right size and the wrong shape

v3/colors.css declares 150 token lines carrying 41 distinct colour values. That is not bloat — shadcn ships 30 tokens on a smaller job, and most of CCD's extra lines are aliases that exist so a stock shadcn component renders correctly without being edited.

The problem is not the count. It is where the values sit: the neutral ramp — the part of a palette that does the most work on the most screens — has five steps and a hole in the middle, and every colour a UI reaches for next falls into that hole.

2 · The neutral ramp

Five steps, and a canyon between two of them

3 · Two things the neutrals disagree about

The blue tilt is inconsistent between modes

Every neutral in the system sits at hue 262–271 — one blue-grey family, matching the navy (263) and the action blue (265). That part is coherent. Chroma is not.

#CBD1DF0.020#2123270.008
#61697A0.029#383B410.011

Light neutrals get bluer as they darken, ×24 from top to bottom — a deliberate-looking technique. Dark neutrals stay flat at ~0.010 throughout. Two different intents, one system. Whichever is right, it should be the same one twice.

Selection and structure are the same colour

v2 ships two tokens here and its own comment says they were split on purpose, “so structure ≠ selection”: --divider #EDEFF1 for hairlines and tracks, --accent-tint #E9F1FC for selected rows and sidebar hover.

#EDEFF1 · #E9F1FC — ΔE 1.4. Below any threshold at which two people would call them different colours. The split was declared and never rendered.

v3 dropped the second token when it adopted shadcn's naming, so it now has one value doing both jobs — more honest, and equally unable to show a selected row. Neither generation can.

4 · One thing worth knowing about the status trio

They are the macOS window buttons

CCDmacOS traffic lightΔE
--positive #27C93Fzoom #28C8400.31
--caution #FFBD2Eminimise #FEBC2E0.30
--negative #FF5F56close #FF5F570.11

Effectively the same three values — v2's own comment calls them “terminal green / yellow / red”, so this was known, if not written down as a decision. They work, they are instantly readable, and the whole -strong / -tint apparatus was built on top of them because none of the three can hold text.

Nothing is broken. It is simply the one part of the palette that is somebody else's and reads as generic-Mac rather than as Boonrawd — worth a decision rather than an inheritance.

5 · Proposal

Fill the gap. Move nothing that exists.

Every value in the ramp today keeps its exact hex. Three new steps go into the hole in each mode, generated to sit on the same hue and to continue the same chroma trend, so they read as part of the ramp rather than as arrivals.

2 · Does the light ramp's growing blue tilt stay? Either dark mode adopts it, or light mode drops it. Right now they contradict each other and nobody has decided which is the house style.

Recommended: take the six new steps now — they are additive, they close a hole that is currently filled by guesswork, and nothing shipped can regress.

Leave the status trio alone. It works, and replacing three known-readable colours with Boonrawd-derived ones is a real project with a real risk, not a tidy-up.
