shadcn — all 7 base themes, in full

Every token of every base theme, light and dark, read from the registry (ui.shadcn.com/r/colors/<name>.json). The specimens carry CCD's brand blue in --primary and --ring; everything else is shadcn's, unmodified.

1 · The headline

They are nearly the same theme

Distance from neutral, measured on the four tokens that carry any tint. Ten is the floor at which two people call something a different colour.

Nothing reaches 5. The largest gap in the whole set is olive's secondary text at 4.0. Backgrounds, cards and popovers are byte-identical across all seven. Choosing a base theme is choosing the temperature of your grey text — not a look.

6 · Reading it

The five chart slots are a grey ramp in all seven themes. L 0.87 / 0.556 / 0.439 / 0.371 / 0.269, tinted to the theme. Sequential, not categorical — so CCD's chart palette has nothing to inherit here no matter which theme is picked.

The eight sidebar tokens point at slightly different values from their page equivalents — --sidebar is one step off --background. CCD's flat rule maps them all back onto the page values, which is why they exist in colors.css at all.

Only --destructive carries real chroma in any of them: 0.245 light, 0.191 dark. It is the single colour shadcn ships.
