Colour systems — same UI, four palettes

Identical markup and identical type in all four. Only the colour tokens change. Values are taken from each system's own source, not approximated.

Look at the card inside the box. Radix and Material tint it — a block of content gets its own background. shadcn and CCD leave it white and separate it with a line only. Measured, not eyeballed: card and page are the same colour in A and D, different in B and C.

So A and D agree on the biggest rule. Where they part is the button row: shadcn's primary is near-black and its “ยกเลิก” is a grey fill, where CCD's primary carries the brand navy-blue and its secondary is white with a line. And CCD's text is navy, not neutral black.
