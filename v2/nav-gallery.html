2 · Seg — in-view value filter, the chosen option lifts out of the trough

Tabs switch a section; seg narrows the one you are in. A seg sits above a table that already draws its own outline, so it adds no border of its own: the trough and the lifted card carry the state. Labels are body-size (at caption size the muted text reads as a broken font), they carry no counts (the view below already answers "how many"), and the active option never changes weight, because bolding widens the advance and reflows the row.
