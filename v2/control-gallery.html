1 · Bar family — var(--ctl-h) · every control that shares a line

This page exists because the pieces below live in four different component files, and nothing ever showed them together — which is how v2 drifted to five different control heights. Any control standing in a bar reads --ctl-h and sets its height outright; it never derives height from padding, because an <input> refuses to shrink below its font’s natural line box.

2 · Row family — var(--ctl-h-sm) · compact tables only

The one exception, with one job: staying near the 22.4px line of text it rides, so a dense table does not loosen. Allowed only inside .tbl--compact. Its label stays --fs-body — 12px was tried and rejected (a caption-size control beside body copy reads as broken, and Thai has a 14px floor).

3 · Deliberately outside — these do not read the token

.st and .check already sit at 22.4px, exactly the text line — that is why they never disturb a row, and why forcing them to a control height would break something that works. .tabs and .menu__item own their own container: nav.css forbids tabs and seg sharing a shape, and a menu item lives inside the floating pane. A textarea is prose, not a control on a line.
