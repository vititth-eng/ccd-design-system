Menu & triggers

A menu is two parts: a trigger you press and a pane that floats open. The DS ships the pane (.menu); the trigger has been hand-rolled in every app. This page reunites them.

1 · The pane — .menu (menu.css). Ships today. This is what every trigger opens.

Slots: .menu__item (button or link) · --on current choice (accent tint) · --danger destructive · .menu__sep divider. Disclosure pattern: trigger toggles .is-open, outside-click / Esc closes.

2 · Triggers — proposed .menu-btn family. Today these are 5 hand-rolled copies across apps.

a · Select-trigger — pick one value
b · Kebab — row / card actions

One face, two shapes. Both are a white .menu-btn (card fill, rule border, r-base) that opens .menu — --kebab is just icon-only. The app-switcher & user menu keep their bespoke triggers in shell.css (shell-specific), not this family.

3 · Why this page exists

The pane was a DS component with no gallery, so it was invisible and got re-implemented (.rmenu) instead of reused. And "button that opens a menu" was never a DS component at all — it lived as .statusbtn, .rchip, the kebab, the app-switcher, the user menu: five copies, zero source of truth. Approve .menu-btn + --kebab and they graduate into menu.css, then .statusbtn and cowork's .rchip retrofit to it — one atomic change.
