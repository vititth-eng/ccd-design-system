4 · Strong tier — a status hue heavy enough to carry meaning without a dot. TWO of the three are text-safe; caution is not.

+12% ↑ เกินกำหนด 3 วัน 5 คน · ต่ำกว่าเกณฑ์

Bright --pos/--cau/--neg are dot-only (fail AA as text). --pos-strong (5.08:1) and --neg-strong (5.34:1) clear the 4.5:1 text bar, so they may be read as text or a number: deltas, overdue days. --cau-strong is 3.40:1 — glyphs and marks only. When a caution needs words, the words are --ink and the hue lives in the glyph beside them; darkening the amber to reach 4.5:1 is what produced the olive it replaced.

The meter never takes a status hue. A self-reported status and a computed progress bar are two signals allowed to disagree (a project can be 90% done and still at risk), so one colour would merge them. Accent only. Default width suits a table cell; .meter--wide is the card-header size.
