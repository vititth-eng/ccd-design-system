Not a CSS component — a standalone HTML file of inline literals. Email strips <style> and cannot resolve var(), so tokens are copied by hand and drift silently. Source: v2/email/invite-base.html · doctrine: wiki/email-playbook.md

Invite base — slots shown literally; two step blocks, second is deletable

Why it is written this way — each rule is a failure that shipped in testing

  • Inline style only — the bgcolor attribute is stripped, and a button coloured that way arrives white-on-white.
  • Fill sits on the <a> itself. A coloured wrapper around a plain anchor loses its colour and keeps the white text.
  • Every color is paired with a background-color on the same element. Unpaired, dark-mode inversion erases it.
  • No HTML comments inside the body — conditional comments are removed together with whatever they wrap, so the VML bulletproof-button pattern leaves a hole.
  • No nested tables. They flatten: a callout table rendered as a small empty square with its text outside.
  • No line begins N.  — Outlook autoformats it into a list and restarts numbering at every button, giving 1. 1. 1.
  • No SVG. PNG at 2× with explicit width/height; the logo carries a decided background, never transparency by accident.
  • Line-height 1.5 and one enclosed block per step — 1.7 with loose steps was reported unreadable in Thai.
