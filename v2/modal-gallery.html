Modal

Native <dialog> · focus trap + ESC + backdrop free · closes on ×, Cancel, ESC, or backdrop click

Modal when the task interrupts: bounded (≤ ~5 fields), safe to discard, and it does not need the page behind it. Creating a thing, confirming a consequence. Inline when the form IS the page's content, or when you fill it in repeatedly (add a task, edit a row) — there the surrounding rows are the context. Never hand-roll a modal as an absolutely-positioned panel: you lose the focus trap, ESC and the backdrop, and you inherit whatever stacking context you were dropped into.

Triggers — click to open

สร้างรอบการประเมิน

A long form grows down, never sideways. The modal caps at min(84vh, 720px), the body scrolls, and the head and foot stay pinned so the title and the confirm button are always on screen. If a form needs more width than 460px to make sense, it is a page, not a modal.
