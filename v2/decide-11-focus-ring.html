Focus ring — 4 options

Each field is shown in its FOCUSED look. Same accent #4068CA. Pick the one that reads as "active" at a glance without shouting.

A · Faint halo (current)

accent border + 3px accent-tint halo

B · Stronger halo

accent border + 3px rgba(accent, .20)

C · Outline offset

accent border + 2px solid outline, 2px gap

D · Border only

accent border, no ring (flattest)

On the 1366 Windows floor, A's halo nearly disappears; D can be missed when the resting border is already visible. B and C both signal clearly — B softer, C crisper/more utilitarian. My lean: B on focus-visible (keyboard), border-only on mouse-click.
