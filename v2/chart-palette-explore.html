Tableau 20 · colour-blind —green↔orange ΔE 3.4 protan fails, no subset fixes it

Tableau 20's real cost is colour-blindness, not contrast. Green and orange collapse onto each other for a protan reader (ΔE 3.4), and I searched every 4-, 5- and 6-hue subset — the best reachable is 4.8, still under the floor. Tableau knows: that is why Color Blind 10 is a separate palette in the same sheet. The mitigation is the one these charts already use — a legend plus a value label on every mark, so hue is never the only thing carrying identity. If that is not enough, the answer is Color Blind 10 and fewer series, not a re-tinted Tableau 20.
